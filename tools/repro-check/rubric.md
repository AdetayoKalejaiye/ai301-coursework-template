# Rubric: is this reproduction package ready to post?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| claim-specific | Candidate claim comment against the issue title, body, and clarifications; Comms in the evidence guide | The claim identifies the particular behavior or discrepancy being investigated and a relevant next investigation step. It commits to reporting observations without guaranteeing a fix or a completion date. Generic offers to help do not pass. | required |
| environment-identifiable | Repro report's environment record and code revision against the issue's affected configuration; Environment in the evidence guide | The report identifies the tested code state and the OS, runtime, dependency, backend, or hardware details needed to interpret and repeat this issue's trigger. Relevant differences from the issue's configuration are explicit. Do not demand unrelated versions or exact patch versions when a stated range identifies the tested target sufficiently. | required |
| steps-rerunnable | Repro report's setup, commands, input, fixture contents, and trigger; Steps in the evidence guide | A stranger can reach the reported observation from the stated starting point without guessing a material command, option, input, or unavailable file. Standard repository setup may be referenced precisely; any departure needed to trigger the issue must be supplied. No minimum step count or prescribed headings are required. | required |
| observable-evidence | Repro report's embedded output, log, traceback, screenshot, file excerpt, or concrete before/after observation; Behavior shown in the evidence guide | The package contains an inspectable artifact or concrete observation from the attempted trigger that supports its reported result. A promise of an attachment, an inaccessible local path, or an assertion such as "same error" alone does not pass. A cannot-reproduce report must show the actual result of its attempt. | required |
| issue-target-matched | Issue body and thread clarifications compared with the trigger, input, and artifacts in the repro report; Behavior shown in the evidence guide | The attempt exercises the behavior and distinguishing conditions described by the issue. Evidence claimed as reproduction shows that behavior, rather than an adjacent error or an earlier setup failure. A cannot-reproduce attempt passes when it attempts the described operation, shows its result, and discloses relevant unmet or uncertain trigger conditions. It need not prove that those conditions were achieved. An unrelated failure or setup failure before the target operation does not pass. | required |
| outcome-supported | Repro report's expected/observed behavior and conclusion against its artifacts and environment; Honesty in the evidence guide | The conclusion states what happened and stays within the evidence. A supported reproduction or an evidenced cannot-reproduce both pass. Unsupported certainty, invented execution, conflating setup failure with the issue, or claiming a cause or fix beyond what was tested fails. | required |
| repo-conventions | Repo-facts block in eval mode; repository contribution guide, report template, AI policy, and applicable issue instructions in live mode, read against both candidate comments; Comms in the evidence guide | The comments supply the applicable requested report information and comply with explicit contribution and AI-use rules, including required AI-assistance disclosure. Equivalent placement is sufficient unless exact formatting is explicitly required. Where the repository explicitly requires AI-use disclosure, require a tool-and-extent disclosure or an explicit no-AI statement rather than assuming unstated provenance. A rule asking for human-written comments alone does not require an authorship certificate: assess the actual comments for specific engagement in the author's own words unless the bundle supplies contrary provenance. Do not invent a disclosure requirement where no policy requires one, or require a second new-issue template for an ordinary thread comment. | required |
| communication-usable | Candidate claim and repro comments against the issue's specifics; Comms in the evidence guide | The comments communicate a relevant investigation and distinguish expected from observed behavior without misleading promises, hostility, pressure on maintainers, or filler that obscures the proof. A short complete report passes; polished boilerplate with no issue-specific substance does not. | required |
| evidence-easy-to-locate | Labels and references surrounding the report's artifacts | A reader can connect each deciding artifact to its reproduction step and conclusion without searching unrelated material. | preferred |

## Verdict rule

Accept only when every applicable required check passes. Reject if any applicable required check fails or is unclear. Preferred checks never change the verdict. Use unclear only when deciding evidence is absent or cannot be verified; use fail when the supplied evidence contradicts the pass condition.

For a claim-only live draft, apply claim-specific, repo-conventions, and communication-usable to the claim alone. Report environment-identifiable, steps-rerunnable, observable-evidence, issue-target-matched, outcome-supported, and evidence-easy-to-locate as unclear with evidence exactly "not yet applicable: claim-only draft" and exclude them from the verdict. A report template's reproduction fields are not required before the investigation occurs. In a full package, apply every check. In eval mode, grade only the frozen bundle and ignore scope.md and voice-guide.md.
